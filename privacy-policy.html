Privacy Policy

StringScribe · DMD Engineering LLC · Last updated: [DATE]

StringScribe turns your guitar playing into tablature. It is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your information.

The short version

StringScribe does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. Your recordings and tabs stay on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising tracking.

Microphone

StringScribe asks for microphone access so it can record your playing. Audio is recorded only while you are recording, is processed on your device to produce the tab, and is saved only if you choose to keep the take. It is never uploaded.

Your recordings and tabs

Takes, tab documents and exported PDFs are stored in the app's folder on your device, which you can see in the Files app. They leave your device only if you share or export them yourself, or if your own iCloud or device backups include them (governed by Apple's privacy policy).

Settings

Your preferences (for example tuning, metronome and display settings) are stored on your device and are not shared.

Purchases

If StringScribe offers purchases, they are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

StringScribe collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

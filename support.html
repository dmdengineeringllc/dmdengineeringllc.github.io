Getting started

Tap the record button, play, and tap it again to stop. Your tab appears while you play and is finished a few seconds after you stop. In the app, ••• › Help explains every feature, including a quick start.

Frequently asked questions

Which guitars does it work with?

Acoustic, classical and electric guitars, in standard or any of the common tunings, with or without a capo. Set the tuning before recording (the tuning button above the tab) so the strings and frets come out right. It transcribes one guitar at a time; with other instruments or singing in the room, results are less accurate.

How do I get the best results?

A quiet room, the device about half a metre (two feet) from the guitar, and the guitar in tune. An audio interface or a clip-on microphone gives the cleanest signal. If you record with the metronome clicking all the way through, use headphones, so the click is not picked up with the guitar.

A note is wrong or missing. Can I fix it?

Yes. Tap Edit (or tap a note) to correct notes, strings, timing and lengths, add missed notes or chords, and mark playing techniques. Play the take with Both to hear your recording and the tab together: a wrong note stands out.

The bars or rhythm look wrong.

The rhythm depends on the tempo. Tap the tempo button and use Tap Along or type the tempo; Half / Double and Set Bar Start fix bars at the wrong speed or in the wrong place. If you play pairs of eighth notes long–short, set Feel to Swing.

How do I change the music itself, not just correct it?

Tap Make Editable Tab. It creates a tab document from your recording that you can rearrange freely: insert notes, chords and rests, change rhythms. Your recording is kept as it was.

Where are my recordings and tabs?

In the app's Library (the folder button), and in the Files app under On My iPhone / On My iPad › StringScribe › Takes. They are included in your device backups.

How do I print or share a tab?

Use Export PDF (the share button on iPad, the ••• menu on iPhone). The Pages view shows exactly what the PDF will look like.

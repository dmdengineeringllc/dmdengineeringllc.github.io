Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a calculator?

  • Take a photo of any calculator
  • Define the display area
  • Tap to define button positions
  • Assign functions to each button

How do I upgrade to Pro?

  • Open Settings in the app
  • Tap "Upgrade to Pro"
  • Complete the in-app purchase

What does Pro include?

  • Remove all advertisements
  • Access to future Pro features
  • Support ongoing development

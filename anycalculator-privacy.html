Privacy Policy

Overview

ANY Calculator is developed and published by DMD Engineering LLC. This privacy policy explains how the app handles user information and data.

Data Collection

ANY Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. No user accounts, analytics, or personal data are gathered by the app itself.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and may use anonymous device identifiers (such as advertising IDs) to serve relevant ads. AdMob may collect and process limited, non-personal information as described in Google’s own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

ANY Calculator offers optional in-app purchases to remove ads or unlock Pro features. Transactions are handled securely by Apple’s App Store. The developer does not have access to any payment information or billing details.

Children’s Privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age.
